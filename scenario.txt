COGNIWORK — DATA ANALYST SIMULATION

Company: ShopPulse Operations

Role: Junior Data Analyst

TASK

Customer churn has suddenly increased.

Your senior analyst has asked you to investigate the customer
dataset and identify possible reasons for customer churn.

You have 10 minutes.

You must:
1. Identify data-quality problems.
2. Find patterns related to churn.
3. State your hypothesis.
4. Explain your reasoning to your senior analyst.
5. Adapt when new information is provided.
6. Give a recommendation to management.


WORKPLACE STORY

9:00 AM

You have joined ShopPulse Operations as a Junior Data Analyst.

Your senior analyst, Alex, sends you a message:

"Good morning. We have noticed an unusual increase in customer
churn this month.

Management wants to know what might be causing it.

I've shared the customer dataset with you.

Please investigate the data and give me your initial findings."


ALEX — SENIOR ANALYST

"What is your initial hypothesis about why customers are leaving?"


UNEXPECTED INCIDENT

URGENT UPDATE FROM OPERATIONS:

"The customer classification system has recently changed.

Some Enterprise customers may currently be labelled differently
in the dataset.

Your previous analysis may therefore be affected."


ALEX:

"Management still needs your revised analysis in 3 minutes.

How will you handle this situation?"


FINAL QUESTION

"Based on your analysis, what should ShopPulse do to reduce
customer churn?"
